--- Iron Soul: Dead God's Dream ---
===================================

Iron Soul is an ambitious permadeath roguelite system for Skyrim with configurable death rules, Dragon Soul resurrection,
and persistent progression, expanding into a multi-character saga built around Heart Shards and escalating undead pressure.

--- Current TODO ---
--------------------

- Add the Heart Shard system.
- Add permanent account-level perk unlocks tied to Heart Shard progress.
- Integrate cinematic dragon soul absorption.


--- Roadmap ---
===============

--- V1: Heart Shards ---
- Introduce Heart Shards of Lorkhan as permanent discoveries that can be found across multiple characters.
- Use Heart Shard progress to unlock account-level perks that persist beyond individual character deaths.
- Add light "Lorkhan whisper" framing around shard discovery without revealing the deeper source of the voice.
- Fold cinematic dragon soul absorption into the progression loop so major supernatural gains feel weighty and visible.

--- V2: Return of the Dead God ---
- Flesh out the history around Lorkhan, the Heart, and the player's growing connection to the shards.
- Reveal that the apparent voice of Lorkhan is actually Dagoth Ur's dream-shadow manipulating the player.
- Expand the Heart mystery into a stronger story layer that recontextualizes the V1 shard hunt.

--- V3: War of the Barrows ---
- Rework Draugnarok raid pressure so the endgame source is Labyrinthian and the active pressure network flows from dragon priest barrows.
- Spawn lower raid categories from the closest active dragon priest barrow instead of generic locations where practical.
- Turn roaming mobs into reinforcement bands that travel from dragon priest barrows to Labyrinthian and guard there until cleaned up.
- Completing Labyrinthian should disable or heavily suppress minor capital raids, capital raids, and gate crashes.
- Killing a dragon priest should disable or reduce the raid pressure linked to that priest's barrow.
- Dragon priest anchors to explore: Morokei at Labyrinthian, Rahgot at Forelhost, Hevnoraak at Valthume, Vokun at High Gate Ruins, Volsung at Volskygge,
  Otar at Ragnvald, Nahkriin at Skuldafn, and Krosis at Shearpoint with possible Korvanjund support routing.
- Restore the kill-handler concept as an animated draugr kill counter that tracks kills by the player, followers, summons, or player-owned actors.
- Build hidden draugr heat from draugr kills and dragon soul absorption, with dragon souls acting as a louder supernatural signal.
- Use higher heat to increase the chance of a draugr assassin or personal vengeance squad during a Draugnarok pulse.
- Add heat decay, cooldowns, post-attack reduction, and loop protection so assassin kills do not recursively create more assassin pressure.
- Explore optional The Restless Dead integration for richer undead visual variety while preserving Dragon Cultist identity and Iron Soul preset eye language.
